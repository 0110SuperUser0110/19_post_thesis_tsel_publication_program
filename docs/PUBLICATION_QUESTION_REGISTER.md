# Publication Question Register

These questions are provisional and require independent protocol review before data collection or interpretation.

## P-RQ1: External-System Use

Under equal-facts and declared practical baselines, how does representation organization affect temporal reconstruction across specified computational systems?

Required before execution: frozen systems and versions, task and response contracts, repeated observations, eligibility rules, scoring, multiplicity plan, provenance capture, and privacy review.

## P-RQ2: Schema Extension and Complexity

Which candidate additions beyond the seven-field TSEL envelope produce nonredundant preservation or interoperability benefit, and at what point do additional fields increase complexity without measurable benefit?

Required before execution: justified candidate facts, versioned schemas, equal controls, parser and storage costs, deterministic recovery measures, and stopping rules.

## P-RQ3: Reproducibility and Inspectability

How consistently does TSEL support inspectable, reproducible, and clearly labeled mock sensory-data representation before downstream computational modeling?

This was previously mislabeled as a thesis RQ. It is isolated here as a possible publication question. Current thesis hashes, labels, manifests, and repository boundaries are quality controls supporting the two approved thesis questions, not observed results for P-RQ3.

## P-RQ4: Alternative Computational Tasks

Under valid leakage-safe task contracts, how does TSEL compare with matched controls for predictive, sequence, non-tokenized, or emerging computational systems?

Required before execution: a task-specific dataset, independent train/test units where applicable, nonleaking targets, equivalent source facts, declared feature transformations, and system-appropriate metrics.
