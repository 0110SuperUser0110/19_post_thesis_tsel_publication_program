# Thesis Boundary

Status date: 2026-08-25

The submitted ARB application and the Board's approval-with-minor-revisions guidance control the thesis. The thesis is a Design Science Research artifact-validation study of a seven-field temporal encoding layer across heterogeneous public or simulated sensory inputs.

The following items are outside the approved thesis research questions:

- performance through free public language-model interfaces;
- superiority over every flat representation or downstream system;
- fields beyond the approved seven-field schema;
- globally optimal schema width;
- actual key-removal variants;
- human usability, biological fidelity, or subjective experience; and
- a standalone reproducibility and inspectability outcome question.

These items may motivate publication studies. They may appear in the thesis only as bounded future work and cannot be used as thesis completion gates, thesis results, or an implied third RQ.
